Fonts bundled in this folder
=============================

- NotoNastaliqUrdu.ttf  (Noto Nastaliq Urdu)
- NotoNaskhArabic.ttf   (Noto Naskh Arabic)
- NotoSansArabic.ttf    (Noto Sans Arabic)

Source: Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com), designed by Google as part
of the Noto font family.

License: SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1)
https://openfontlicense.org

These fonts are bundled so the synthetic data generator
(`src/synthetic.py`) works offline / inside Colab without an extra
download step. They give three visually distinct Urdu writing styles
(Nastaliq / calligraphic, Naskh / book, Sans / modern-digital) which is
exactly the "different fonts" variety the Week 1 and Week 3 handouts ask
for.

You are free to drop in additional Urdu fonts (e.g. Jameel Noori Nastaleeq,
Alvi Nastaleeq) into this folder — `src/synthetic.py` will pick up every
`.ttf`/`.otf` file placed here automatically.
